Major update

The island keeps changing, and these shores
are nothing like you remember.

Thirteen days since Progress! This update brings a garden of creatures to raise and a high street in Rokken. There are ten monuments, new or rebuilt, and new things living in the grass. There are grenades and base upgrades. And the server now has the final say.

Presenting

Your first visitor's guide
to the Oumo Garden

A floating island over the clouds where small soft creatures live, and where you raise them.

Raise one in the
GGarden

1The Hatchery

Eggs come from Stitches. You pick a boy or a girl in one of eight coats.

Neutral, Outline, Light and Night eggs each hatch a different look. The spots on the shell tell you its class.

Set the egg on the lawn and wait. The garden lays wild ones of its own, too.

Super rareShiny
One egg in 256 hatches a

Yarn Oumo

It comes out knitted, with a seam and a pom-pom, to sparkles and a chime instead of a pop. It hatches at level 40 with every grade a step up.

2The Lawn

Oumos crawl until level 10, then walk, and sometimes fall flat on their faces. Tap E to pet one, hold E to pick it up, and press E again to hug it.

Love turns into experience, and hunger slows it down. At level 30 an Oumo spins a cocoon and comes out grown up. At 90 a gold cocoon makes it Ultima.

GrowingUp
What it eats is what it becomes

Gun, Magic, Health

Its class shows on its head. GUN Oumos grow swept horns, MAGIC ones a crest, and HEALTH ones drooping leaf ears. Girls are slimmer and have their own softer shapes.

3The Orchard

There are three trees, one for each class. Fruit drops when it is ripe. Shake a tree and everything comes down, ripe or not.

One fruit in 164 is Rainbow, which feeds all three classes. Another one in 164 is Gold, which doubles levelling for half an hour. Hungry Oumos go and find fallen fruit for themselves. Walk past with one in your hand and they beg.

4The Imbuer

Carry an Oumo onto the machine. At the terminal you can store its soul in a glass tank, put it on a gun, or carry it as an item. If the gun breaks, the soul comes home.

An Oumo can also sit on a base turret and toughen it, by up to 45% for an Ultima.

BornAgain
Twenty crystals make an

Elemental Oumo

Take an Ultima to level 99 and the Imbuer can make it a child again. It keeps its name and its clothes, and earns a gold star and better grades.

Only a reborn Oumo eats crystals. Twenty of them make it Fire, Frost, Thunder, Water, Wind, Earth, Light or Dark, with skin made of the element.

5The Blue Grotto

A glowing sea cave under the garden. Follow the GROTTO signs and the vines part as you walk through. A wide arch joins the east bay to the nursery.

An Oumo raised down here leans towards MAGIC.

Say hello toNew
Feet in the water

Tori

Tori, a dolphin woman, sits on the edge of the little islet in the lagoon. She doesn't say much, and seems happy just to be there. A skyhook over the islet takes you back up to the ledge.

6Stitches

A little floating island next door, two skyhooks away. Stitches sells eggs, furniture for the lawn, and clothes for your Oumo.

There are hats, bows, shirts, skirts, headphones and cat ears. Each piece adds a little turret resist. Set your Oumo on the changing plinth on the lawn to dress it, and rename it at the counter.

Behind the counterNew
She'll measure you up

Marina

A shark who runs a tailor's. A beret Oumo waves at you from her counter, and nine dressed dolls sit on the shelves behind her.

Furnish theLawn

Decor

There's a teddy, an old TV, a bookshelf, a beach ball, a skull and a pumpkin. There's also the Drith Playset, a little base that scrap minions attack while tiny turrets pop them.

Oumos read at the shelf, watch the TV together, and cheer at the playset.

Skyhooks are brass balls with glowing faces that hover and spin. Hookshot one and it carries you across, hanging underneath. They link the Garden, Stitches and the Grotto.

Across from Pitcher's. All twelve base modules are here, plus Nightblade moves and passives.

The movement abilities are here too. Buy High Jump, Double Jump, Quick Run and Glide, then come back and take each one to level 3.

10Round the piazza

Forgie's Forge has moved off the Caldera into a glass-fronted shop on the piazza, and Gideon is still on the ceiling.

Weaver's, the armour shop, is a weavers' guild now, with working spinning wheels, a loom and a dye cauldron. The Potion Shop has jars up to the beams.

Number 11

The Bubble Stand

Pay 250 Monny and it blows a bubble with the money inside. A minute or three later the bubble drifts down somewhere on the island. Whoever shoots it open gets the Monny, and a note saying who it's from.

Corey's Sundries

Look before you buy

Press E on a case and you get a slip with the item's picture, its stats and its cost. Nothing is bought until you say yes, and empty cases say SOLD OUT. The tent also turns up on eight pads round the Caldera now.

The Caldera 12 13 14 15 16 17
Plant a flag on the
CCaldera

Ten monuments are new or rebuilt. They're the places worth going, which means everyone else is going too.

12The Tower

A granite war memorial on the Caldera's highest summit. The beacon on top turns red and blue, and you can see it across the island. Inside are a memorial chamber and a hundred steps up to the lookout.

13The Radio Tower

Sixty-four metres of red-and-white steel lattice, with three ladders and three decks. There's a nyxite chest at the bottom and on every deck, and each one is rarer than the last: Uncommon in the shelter, Rare, Ultra, and Secret at the very top.

14The Oil Rigs

A drilling platform with a derrick over a moonpool. There are two, one off the east coast and one off the west. Each has red and blue ends that mirror each other, with a helideck, a crane, a flare boom and a boat dock. The ghost ship ties up alongside.

15The Ice Cavern

A winding ramp goes seventeen metres down to a hall of ice pillars and a frozen pool. An ice door opens on a vault full of stars, with a chest inside. It is the one monument you're allowed to build a base near.

16The Sawmill

King of the hill in a lumber camp. The mill and its saw deck stand in the middle, with a blue camp to the north and a red one to the south. Each camp has sheds, log decks and burners.

17The Post

A five-storey post office with a forecourt. Go through the mirror-ball gallery and you come out on the sorting floor, dark and long abandoned.

Seven Heart Shards lie on rings of light around the grove. Bring all seven to the shack in the north bailey and set them in its stained-glass window. The door opens on three chests for everyone there, and the shards go back out for next time.

Eight sandstone foundations are scattered over the Caldera. Corey's tent and the bomb-flip vault each claim a free one, scanned in as a hologram by the robot crew. Five minutes later they're gone again.

The Monarca Works are out there too: twenty compounds with steel strongboxes to crack.

The new kit

Items, upgrades, and things to put on the base.

Pull the pin

Grenades

Frag, Fire, EMP, Cluster and Smoke. A frag's fuse starts on the first bounce, and fire burns the ground for eight seconds. A cluster lets go six bomblets. Smoke screens for about twelve seconds.

EMP doesn't hurt anyone. Turrets stop firing for eight seconds and shields come off. Live grenades glow in their own colour, and holding aim draws the throw and a landing ring.

For the Module Bay

Twelve cartridges

Firepower, Rapid Fire, Targeting, Plating, Shield, Repair, Overclock, Fuel Saver, Smelter, Airstrike, Barracks and Supply. Each one goes from Mk I to Mk V.

Overclock powers up to five more guns. Shield takes up to 80% off the damage your turrets take.

Sold at Upgrades and Corey's
On the roof

C-RAM and plasma

The CR-01 C-RAM is a roof Gatling. It shoots down rockets, missiles, shells, bombs and anyone flying a minicopter over your base. Plasma cannons come as twins, triples and quads, and there are seven new power cores to run it all.

Missile racks lock on with a count now, one chime per missile, then fire every tube at once. Their reloads are halved.

What's inside

Chests

A nyxite chest promises one item of its rarity, and its crystal glows that rarity's colour. The steel chest has a real lid now. It swings open, and stays open once you've emptied it.

Climb into the light at the top of the Tower of Babel and you come out on a still sea under open sky, with three winged chests waiting.

Something to wear

Jet Boots and two hats

Jet Boots replace the fastest running shoes, with fire at the heels. There are two pirate hats, the Corsair's Plume and the Cabin Hat. The Field set (tunic, hose and boots) joins the rack too.

Collect them all

The Blueprint Reader

It's a codex now. Every blueprint is a card, locked, held or decoded, and there's a count of how many you've decoded this wipe. There are six milestones of Renown and bonuses for finishing a set. Learn one and the fetch bot goes to get it.

Forged with a crystal

Crystals and ingots

Silver forged with a crystal makes a Gun, Magic or Health ingot, and Oumos will eat those too. There's also the AP Boost for an extra Ability Point this wipe, and Genesis Blisters, where more than half the pulls are Rare or better.

And under the hood

Also new

Moving

  • The double jump is an aerial dodge now, once per jump.
  • Keep holding dash through a roll and it turns into Quick Run.
  • Hold dash or guard in the air to Glide, and let go to drop.
  • Every hit lands on you now: you flinch, slide, get launched and land. Aerial Recovery catches you.
  • Swimming has its own crawl and tread. You float, and the seabed isn't a floor.
  • Running on the open Caldera is faster.

Online

  • Dedicated servers only, with our own server browser. You need the server's exact build to join.
  • The server has the final say on packs, Monny, bases, clans, shops, loot, PvP damage and movement. Bad moves snap back.
  • The netcode is packed and thinned by distance, so a server can hold 64 players.
  • You see other players' facing, swings, shots, reloads and spells.

Everything else

  • The moon favours one class each night. A ring on the clock shows which.
  • Item stats are drawn as rows of cells.
  • The player wears a bold white outline.
  • Teleporters hum, wind up and throw orbs while you wait.
  • The Garden's waterfall runs down rapids, under a plank bridge, into the aqueduct.
