Progress!

Four monuments rise as the Caldera fills up

It has been ten weeks since the introduction. Last time I said the world was the least finished part of the game, so that is where most of the time went. Everything pictured here is the game's own model, rendered out of Blender.

Presenting

A visitor's guide to the Caldera

Four new monuments. They are the places worth going, which means everyone else is going to them too.

Open late

Eddie's Deli

A corner deli with a parking lot out front and a fenced yard round the back. The door is open, there is a bell on it, and the attic fan turns.

It has its own row on the chart, so you can find it when you need it.

The big one

Venaria Reale

The Reggia with the Galleria Grande, its garden, and a small Piedmontese town beside it. The gallery floor is black-and-white marble laid on the diagonal.

The town has a trattoria, and the trattoria plays its own music.

South coast

Beach Town

A Fire Island boardwalk village. There is a ferry pier on the inlet and a dune between the cottages and the sea.

Mind the towers

Forest

A walled sanctuary grove with a shrine in the middle and two watchtowers at each end.

And now

Meet the locals

Not everything on the Caldera is trying to kill you. These ones mostly are not.

Keeps the secret shops

Big Al

Asleep until you walk in. Then he tips his hat and his head follows you round the room. He stays behind the grate.

Runs the smithy

Forgie

A frog in a top hat. Forgie's Forge is his.

Cleans the ceiling

Gideon

A dart frog in plunger boots with a bucket and a sponge. He cleans the ceiling of Forgie's Forge and he is never going to finish.

Lives a cat's day

Windshield

A tuxedo cat by the Caldera spawn. Sits, washes, looks about, goes for a short stroll and comes home.

Fishing advice

Gulliver

A gull perched by the spawn. He will tell you how to fish, but only in riddles.

Some assembly required

Minions

A wave of janky scrap robots a base can muster onto a monument.

Speaking of you

The player

A new body, and a new way of moving it.

The player is a black figure now: a round head, two crooked antennae, clawed hands, three-toed feet and yellow eyes that are always lit. Nothing in the world lights him, so he is as black at noon as he is at midnight.

He moves differently too. Shift on the ground is a dodge roll. A swing, a guard or a hit puts him in a battle stance, and he stands down two seconds after the fight does. Out of a fight he runs with the blade across his shoulders, and with nothing in the pack his hand is empty.

Bring something

The rack

Guns that were boxes are models now, and there are more of them.

Formerly the Schneider

The Elite

The sniper rifle has a new name and a chassis-rifle model to go with it.

Belt-fed

Cleaver & Havoc

The two machine guns get their own models. The cover, the belt and the handle all move when they fire, reload and run dry.

For the base

Howitzer & missile launchers

The howitzer wears a new model in place of the one that never tracked or fired. The missile launchers are a family now: one, two, four and eight tubes.

Overhead

Apache & minicopter

The Apache has a modelled hull with a chin turret, swivelling pods and bomb-bay doors. It parks overhead before it attacks, and a few magazines will bring it down.

The minicopter is yours. Fourteen bolt-on parts are modelled for it.

Or skip the guns

Nightblades & staffs

There are 26 Nightblades and 25 staffs. The one in your hand is the one in your pack.

While you're here

Also new

Series 01

Genesis

The first card pack: 31 cards, and a binder that outlives the wipe. You redeem them at the Genesis Reader in Corey's Sundries.

  • Landing Stones. You choose where you land: north, east, south or west beach.
  • The secret shops. Past Big Al there is a steel door that says TOP SECRET, and a black market behind it.
  • The Sluice Works. The lava room is three basins with lava for two, and five wheels that move which one is dry.
  • Scratch-off tickets. Paper ones. You scratch them.
  • The chart. Airdrops, caches and the ghost ship show as medallions.
  • Haven is rebuilt as a mirrored mining town, and the Baths float on a giant rock over a sea of cloud.
  • Item icons are rendered pictures of the models.
  • Under the hood. No game GDScript is left, every sound is Ogg Vorbis, and the first-use freezes are warmed away on the loading screen.
